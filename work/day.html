Day

Client Microsoft Studio Red Paper Heart Role Unity Developer Tools Unity · Real-time Sensor Data

Day is a digital sculpture shaped by 24 hours of real-time data collected from thousands of sensors throughout the building. Inspired by the intricate nature of the smart building system — functioning much like a living organism that senses and responds to internal and external changes — our team designed a generative form that breathes with the data.

Each hour, a new ring emerges, reflecting the latest sensor data, with each data source influencing distinct elements of the ring's shape. Over 24 hours, these rings come together to create a complete visual representation of a day in the life of the building.
